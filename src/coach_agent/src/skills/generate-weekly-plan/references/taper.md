# Taper / race preparation

- Goal: reduce fatigue while retaining race-specific rhythm and confidence.
- Reduce volume from the absorbed baseline according to race proximity; do not compensate for missed master-plan volume.
- Keep only brief, controlled race-pace touches or relaxed strides. Reduce repetition count before changing familiar pace.
- Preserve explicit rest and avoid novel workouts, strength overload, or maximal-speed sessions.
- The race or tune-up milestone determines the week structure and fueling rehearsal.
